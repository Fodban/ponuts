How to buy $PONUTS

Robinhood Chain is a newer EVM network, so a couple of extra steps vs. a typical buy.

  1. Get an EVM wallet

    Install MetaMask or another EVM-compatible wallet on your phone or browser.

  2. Add Robinhood Chain

    Add Robinhood Chain to your wallet (Chain ID 4663) — most wallets will prompt you automatically the first time you connect to a Robinhood Chain app like Uniswap.

  3. Bridge or fund ETH

    You'll need ETH on Robinhood Chain for gas and swapping. Bridge it over or fund it directly through your wallet provider.

  4. Swap on Uniswap

    Paste the contract address below into Uniswap, enter the amount of ETH to spend, and confirm the swap.
